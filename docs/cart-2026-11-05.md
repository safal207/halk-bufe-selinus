# Basket completion and optional pairings

The basket now keeps the counter handoff visible: selected-food photos, included-side notes, a total on the primary button, and one optional pairing with its price and the resulting order total. The action still displays a counter summary; this static demo does not take online payment or send an order.

## Pairing rules

- A burger without fries or a confirmed plated/children's meal can suggest fries for 120 ₺.
- Food without a drink can suggest tea for 10 ₺. The four plated chicken dishes and both children's meals already include fries, so they never receive a duplicate-fries suggestion.
- Drinks without food can suggest revani for 125 ₺.
- An empty or unpriced basket gets no offer. Existing items are not suggested again. Accepting an offer shows a compact confirmation with Undo rather than a new upsell.
- The projected total includes all current quantities plus exactly one optional item. Nothing is preselected or added until the guest chooses it. Undo removes one offered unit, including after a manual quantity increase.

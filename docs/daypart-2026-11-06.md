# Time-aware featured meals

The homepage now selects a featured dish and its photograph using the venue's `Europe/Istanbul` clock. This is a presentation schedule, not a statement about opening hours or live availability.

| Gazipaşa time | Featured dish | Menu price | Photograph |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 05:00–10:59 | Kahvaltı tabağı | 220 ₺ | Soft morning sunlight |
| 11:00–16:59 | Tavuk şiş | 290 ₺ | Clear natural daylight |
| 17:00–04:59 | Tavuk ızgara | 320 ₺ | Warm evening light |

Automatic mode is the default. The time selector previews Morning / Daytime / Evening; `?time=morning`, `?time=day` and `?time=evening` preserve the selected preview in the URL. Choosing Auto removes that override. Theme and language choices remain independent. The rest of the menu remains available in every period.

The minute-aligned clock updates an open page at the boundary and checks again after tab visibility or page restoration. It updates only the featured meal: title, photo, price, portion note, add button, nutrition link and loading-screen meal. It does not replace the basket or close an open dish. Existing cart totals and selected products stay unchanged.

## Assets

Three photo edits were made with the built-in image generator, preserving each original dish and its sides. They are saved as `assets/daypart/{morning,day,evening}-{640,1280}.webp`; the original catalog assets remain intact. Exact prompts and source references are in `daypart-prompts.json`. These are serving illustrations, as labelled in the UI, rather than photographs of actual restaurant service.
